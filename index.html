Research

Automation, Unionization, and Worker Welfare
with A. Arda Gitmez
Working Paper

Automation pulls on workers in two directions. On the one hand, it can depress wages and weaken unions; on the other, productivity gains can expand labor-market opportunities that indirectly benefit workers.

We develop a model to study when each force dominates and examine policies designed to protect workers while preserving the benefits of technological change.
